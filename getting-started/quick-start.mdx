Connect the two systems that already know your people and devices, and June builds your roster and inventory from them. Everything below is done by an Owner or Admin.

## 1. Sign in

Go to the June login page, enter your work email and click **Continue**. Depending on your email, June asks for your password, or sends you straight to your company's single sign-on page. You can also choose **Google**.

If you are the first person from your company, June asks you to create your organization. Enter a name and pick an account type. You become the organization's **Owner**.

<Note>
New organizations are reviewed before they open. Until then you see **Organization Pending**, and June notifies you when it is ready.
</Note>

After you sign in, June opens **Agent**, where you can ask questions in plain language once your systems are connected.

## 2. Connect your identity provider

Your identity provider creates your employees, along with their departments. Connect it first, so devices can be matched to their owners by email.

In June, go to **Settings** > **Integrations**, select the tool under **Available Integrations**, and click **Connect**. Google Workspace, Microsoft Entra and Slack send you to the provider to approve access. Tools that use credentials ask for them on the same page. Each integration page above lists exactly what to enter.

## 3. Connect your device tool

Your device tool brings in laptops, phones and tablets, and who each one is assigned to.

  <Card title="Jamf Pro" icon="/images/jamf_logo.png" href="/integrations/jamf-pro">Sync managed Apple devices</Card>
  <Card title="Kandji" icon="/images/kandji.svg" href="/integrations/kandji">Sync managed Apple devices</Card>
  <Card title="Apple Business Manager" icon="/images/abm-dark-mode.svg" href="/integrations/apple-business-manager">Sync enrollment and purchase data</Card>
</CardGroup>

Connect these the same way: **Settings** > **Integrations**, select the tool, click **Connect**. Intune devices arrive through Microsoft Entra, so if you connected Entra in step 2 you are already covered. See [Microsoft Intune](/integrations/microsoft-intune).

<Note>
Device tools are available when your plan includes Assets.
</Note>

## 4. See your people and devices

June starts the first sync as soon as a connection succeeds, then syncs once a day. To watch it, open the integration under **Settings** > **Integrations** and check its sync status. **Last sync** reads **Never** until the first sync finishes.

When it completes:

- Open **Assets** > **All Assets** for your devices. See [Assets](/assets/assets).
- Open **Employees** > **All Employees** for your people. See [Employees](/people/employees).

Need fresh data sooner? On the integration page, open the status menu at the top and click **Sync Now**.

## 5. Connect Slack (optional)

Slack lets June post IT notifications and lets people get help from June in Slack. Connect it from **Settings** > **Integrations** under **Collaboration**. See [Slack](/integrations/slack).

<Card title="Slack" icon="/images/slack.svg" href="/integrations/slack">Notifications, requests and help from June in Slack</Card>

## 6. Invite your team

Go to **Settings** > **Members**, click **Invite Member**, enter an email address and choose a role. Roles, single sign-on and email domains are covered in [Organization setup](/getting-started/organization-setup).

## 7. Tell June about your company (optional)

Under **Settings** > **Agent** > **Context**, write the standing knowledge you want June to follow, such as how your team handles requests. See [Context and memory](/agents/context-and-memory).

## If something looks wrong

| What you see | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| Devices or people are missing | Open the integration under **Settings** > **Integrations**. Check that it shows **Connected** and that sync status is not **Sync failed**. Then click **Sync Now**. |
| The integration shows **Not Connected** | Open the status menu and click **Test Connection**. For Google Workspace, Microsoft Entra and Slack, use **Reconnect**. For credential-based tools, click **Edit** under **Connection Settings**, correct the credentials and click **Save changes**. |
| You cannot see **Integrations** in Settings | Connecting integrations needs the Owner or Admin role. Ask one of them. |
| A device has no owner | Connect your identity provider and run **Sync Now** on both tools. June matches people by email. |
