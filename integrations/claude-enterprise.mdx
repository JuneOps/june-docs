This is the recommended way to connect Claude. If you use the Anthropic Console or API instead, see [Claude Platform](/integrations/claude-platform).

## What you'll accomplish
- Connect with a single claude.ai API key
- Track **billed** Claude spend over time, by model, by department, and per person
- See every Claude seat, including people who have access but no usage (the seats you may be paying for and not using)
- Identify top users and the teams driving usage
- Get per-person totals that reconcile to your Anthropic bill
- See each person's spend limit beside their actual spend, and update it from June

## What June syncs

From your Claude Enterprise account, June syncs:

- **Billed spend per person**, across **all** Claude products (chat, Code, and more)
- **Token usage per person**
- **Per-person engagement**: sessions, conversations, and coding output (pull requests, commits) per person per day, split by product
- **Skill usage**: which skills your team uses, how often, and the spend attributed to each
- **Your member roster**, so June can show every seat, including people with no usage
- **Per-person spend limits**, so you can compare each person's cap against what they actually spend

Because every figure is billed and attributed to a person, your per-person and per-department totals reconcile to your Anthropic invoice. Usage history is available from January 2026 onward and refreshes daily.

<Note>
Claude Enterprise uses **one key**. It reads your spend, usage, seats, and each person's spend limit, and lets admins update a limit from June.
</Note>

## Capabilities

June ingests usage, billing, and spend limit data from Anthropic. The only action it can take in your Claude account is updating a spend limit when an admin approves the change in June.

| Capability | Description |
|------------|-------------|
| **Billed spend** | Your authoritative Anthropic invoice total over the selected period, net of account credits. |
| **Spend over time** | Cumulative spend and token usage by model or department across the period. |
| **Per-person spend (billed)** | Billed spend and tokens attributed to each employee, across all Claude products. Searchable, sortable, and groupable by department. |
| **Seat coverage** | Every Claude seat in one view, including unused (idle) seats. Surfaces who has access but no usage, and people who left but still hold a seat. |
| **Per-department & per-model breakdowns** | Where spend concentrates, plus top users. |
| **Engagement & adoption** | Who uses Claude, on which products, and how deeply: sessions, messages, and coding output per person. |
| **Skill usage** | Which skills the team runs, how often, and what each costs per use. |
| **Credits reconciliation** | Used spend minus account credits equals your billed total. |
| **Spend limits** | Each person's spend limit beside their actual spend, so you can spot who is close to their cap and who has no cap set. Admins can also update a limit from June. |

## What each scope is for

All five scopes live on your one key.

| Scope | What June needs it for |
|-------|------------------------|
| `read:analytics` | Reads your Claude spend and usage: the totals, per-person breakdowns, and engagement data behind every chart and table. |
| `read:spend_limits` | Reads each person's configured spend limit, so June can show it beside what they actually spend. |
| `write:spend_limits` | Lets admins update a person's spend limit from June, once an admin approves the change. |
| `read:compliance_user_data` | Reads your member roster, so every seat shows, including people with no usage. |
| `read:compliance_org_data` | Reads your organization's group structure, so June can attribute seats to the right team. |

<Note>
June only reads your **member directory** with the compliance scopes: who is in the organization and which groups they belong to. It never requests conversation content.
</Note>

## Prerequisites

Before starting, ensure you have:
- A Claude **Enterprise** (claude.ai) plan, with the **Compliance API** enabled for your organization
- **Primary Owner** access to your Claude Enterprise parent organization, to create a key carrying every scope June needs.

## Connect Claude Enterprise

<Steps>
  <Step title="Generate your Claude API key">
    This key lets June read your spend, usage, seats, and each person's spend limit, and lets admins update a limit from June.
    1. Sign in to [claude.ai](https://claude.ai) as the **Primary Owner** of your Claude Enterprise parent organization. Only the parent organization's Primary Owner can create a key with every scope June needs.
    2. Go to [**Organization settings → API**](https://claude.ai/admin-settings/api-access) and open the **Keys** section.
    3. Click **Create key**, name it, and select all five scopes: **`read:analytics`**, **`read:spend_limits`**, **`write:spend_limits`**, **`read:compliance_user_data`**, and **`read:compliance_org_data`**.
    4. Copy the secret (it starts with `sk-ant-api01-`) and store it somewhere safe. Anthropic shows it once; if you close the dialog first, you'll need to create a new key.

    Scopes are set when you create the key and can't be changed afterward, so select all five now.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add the integration in June">
    1. In June, open the **Integrations** page.
    2. Choose **Claude Enterprise**.
    3. Paste your key, then save.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Wait for the first sync">
    June backfills your historical usage and refreshes daily. Spend, per-person data, and seats appear under **AI usage → Claude** once the first sync completes.
  </Step>
</Steps>
