## What Gets Synced

| Data | Description |
|------|-------------|
| **Spend** | Your spend over the selected period, net of account credits where the provider reports them |
| **Spend over time** | Cumulative spend and token usage across the period |
| **By model & department** | Where spend concentrates across models and teams |
| **Per person** | Spend, token usage, and engagement attributed to each employee |
| **Spend limits** | Per-person caps read from providers that enforce them, shown beside actual spend |

<Note>
What June can attribute per person depends on the connection. **Claude Enterprise** gives billed, per-person spend across all products. **Claude Platform** gives your billed organization total plus an estimated per-person figure for Claude Code. **Cursor** gives billed, per-person spend with no estimates. **OpenAI Platform** gives billed, per-person spend for your OpenAI API usage, with shared or automated usage shown at the organization level. **ChatGPT Enterprise** gives seats, per-person engagement and spend limits, and estimated per-person spend split by product. See each page for details.
</Note>
