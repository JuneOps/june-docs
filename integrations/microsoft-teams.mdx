Connect Microsoft Teams with June to receive notifications as Adaptive Cards in a Teams channel.

<Info>
Microsoft Teams is a **notification channel**. June uses it to post notifications to the channel you choose. Employees can't chat with June or send requests in Teams; use [Slack](/integrations/slack) for that. It does not manage devices or import tickets.
</Info>

## What you'll accomplish

- Grant admin consent for the June app in your Microsoft tenant
- Install the June app in the team you want to use
- Choose the team and channel June posts notifications to

## Prerequisites

Before starting the integration, ensure you have:

- A **Microsoft Entra (Azure AD) admin** who can grant tenant-wide admin consent
- Permission to install apps into the Microsoft Teams team you want to use
- June organization admin permissions

<Note>
June records your Microsoft Tenant ID when the admin grants consent. There is nothing to copy or paste.
</Note>

## How June Works with Teams

When you connect Teams, an administrator grants consent for the June app in your tenant. June then:

- **Reads the list of teams and channels** so you can pick where notifications go
- **Matches Teams users to your employees** by reading the users in your directory
- **Posts notifications as Adaptive Cards** to the channel you select

### Capabilities

| Capability | Description |
|------------|-------------|
| **Notifications** | June posts alerts and updates as Adaptive Cards to your chosen Teams channel |

## Setup Process

### Step 1: Grant Admin Consent

<Steps>
  <Step title="Start the connection from June">
    In June, go to **Settings** > **Integrations**, select **Microsoft Teams** under **Available Integrations**, and click **Connect** to begin the Microsoft consent flow.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Approve the June app">
    1. Sign in with a Microsoft account that can grant **admin consent** for your tenant
    2. Review the permissions June requests: listing teams, channels and users, and posting messages to channels
    3. Click **Accept** to grant consent for your organization

    <Warning>
    Admin consent must be granted by a tenant administrator. If you are not an admin, the consent screen will ask you to request approval from one before you can continue.
    </Warning>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Return to June">
    Microsoft redirects you back to June and your **Tenant ID** is recorded. The connection is now associated with your Microsoft tenant.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Step 2: Install the June App in Your Team

The June app must be present in the specific team you want notifications in, so it has permission to post there.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Add June to the team">
    1. In Microsoft Teams, open the team you want to use
    2. Go to **Manage team** > **Apps** > **More apps**
    3. Find the **June** app and add it to the team
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Step 3: Choose a Team and Channel

<Steps>
  <Step title="Select where notifications go">
    1. In June, open **Microsoft Teams** under **Configured Integrations**
    2. Under **Microsoft Teams notifications** > **Notifications channel**, choose the **Team**
    3. Choose the **Channel** within that team

    <Check>
    June saves your choice as soon as you pick a channel and shows "Notifications channel saved".
    </Check>
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Troubleshooting

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="No teams or channels appear in the dropdown">
    **Solutions**:
    - Confirm admin consent was granted for your tenant during connection
    - Verify the integration shows as **Connected**; if it doesn't, choose **Reconnect** from its menu
    - If one team is missing, install the June app in that team first
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Notifications fail with a permissions error (403)">
    **Symptoms**: June can list teams but posting fails

    **Solutions**:
    - The June app must be installed in the specific team you selected. Add it via **Manage team** > **Apps**.
    - Re-select the team and channel after installing the app
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Connection shows disconnected">
    **Solutions**:
    - Tenant consent may have been revoked in Microsoft Entra. Choose **Reconnect** from the integration's menu in June.
    - Confirm the June app hasn't been removed from your tenant or team
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Security Best Practices

- **Tenant-scoped consent**: June operates only within the tenant that granted consent
- **Least privilege**: June lists teams, channels and users, and posts only to the channel you choose
- **Public vs. secret values**: June stores your Tenant ID, which is a public identifier, and no secret from your tenant. June requests short-lived access tokens when it needs them.
- **Revoke anytime**: Remove the June app from a team, or revoke consent in Microsoft Entra, to cut off access immediately
