<Info>
June connects to Microsoft through a **single Microsoft Entra integration** that syncs both your identity (employees) and device data. You do not need two separate connections. See the [Microsoft Entra integration](/integrations/microsoft-entra).
</Info>

Connect your Microsoft Intune environment with June for comprehensive device management across Windows, macOS, iOS, and Android devices.

## What you'll accomplish
- Connect Microsoft Entra to June with a single admin-consent sign-in
- Review the Microsoft permissions June asks for
- Understand cross-platform device synchronization

## Prerequisites

Before starting the integration, ensure you have:
- Microsoft Entra ID with Intune licensing
- A **Global Administrator** role, to grant consent for the permissions listed on the [Microsoft Entra integration](/integrations/microsoft-entra#permissions) page
- The Admin or Owner role in June

## Setup Process

### Step 1: Connect Microsoft Entra in June

<Steps>
  <Step title="Access June Integrations">
    1. Log into June
    2. Go to **Settings** > **Integrations**
  </Step>
  
  <Step title="Select Microsoft Entra">
    1. Select **Microsoft Entra** under **Available Integrations** (in the **Identity Providers** group)
    2. Click **Connect** to start setup
  </Step>
  
  <Step title="Complete Microsoft Sign-in">
    1. Sign in with your Microsoft Entra **Global Administrator** account
    2. Grant consent for the requested permissions.
    3. On success, you return to June with the integration active, and the first sync starts.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Step 2: Review What June Syncs

<Steps>
  
  <Step title="Sync Schedule">
    June syncs your directory and Intune devices once a day. To sync sooner, open the **Connected** menu on the integration's page and select **Sync Now**.
  </Step>
  
  <Step title="Capabilities">
    The **Capabilities** section on the integration's page lists what June syncs:
    - Sync all employees (always on)
    - Sync locations
    - Sync all devices (Intune device inventory and specifications)
    - Sync Software Applications
    
    It also lists the device actions June can take in Intune: restart, shut down, force a check-in, and delete a device.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Data Synchronization

### Supported Device Types

| Platform | Device Types | Key Data Synced |
|----------|-------------|-----------------|
| **Windows** | Desktops, Laptops, Tablets | Hardware specs, compliance status, encryption, installed apps |
| **macOS** | MacBooks, iMacs, Mac minis | System info, compliance status, encryption, installed apps |
| **iOS/iPadOS** | iPhones, iPads | Device model, OS version, compliance status, supervision |
| **Android** | Phones, Tablets | Device info, compliance status, security patch level |

## Troubleshooting

### Authentication Issues

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Connection Fails After Sign-in">
    **Symptoms**: You return to June with an error, or the integration shows **Not Connected**
    
    **Solutions**:
    - Sign in with a **Global Administrator** account
    - Confirm admin consent was granted
    - Select **Reconnect** from the integration's menu to approve again
  </Accordion>
  
  <Accordion title="Insufficient Permissions">
    **Symptoms**: "Access denied" or "Forbidden" errors when syncing
    
    **Solutions**:
    - Ensure admin consent was granted for all permissions
    - Check if consent for June was removed in your tenant
    - Verify the user granting consent was a Global Administrator
    - Select **Reconnect** from the integration's menu to approve again
  </Accordion>
  
  <Accordion title="Tenant Access Issues">
    **Symptoms**: "Tenant not found" or "Invalid tenant" errors
    
    **Solutions**:
    - Sign in with an administrator account in the tenant you want to connect
    - Check if tenant has appropriate licensing
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

### Sync Problems

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Missing Devices">
    **Possible Causes**:
    - Devices not enrolled in Intune
    - Insufficient permissions to access device data
    
    **Solutions**:
    - Verify device enrollment status in Intune portal
    - Confirm admin consent covers all the permissions June asks for
  </Accordion>
  
  <Accordion title="Outdated Device Information">
    **Possible Causes**:
    - Devices not syncing with Intune regularly
    - The last June sync ran before the change
    - Network connectivity issues
    
    **Solutions**:
    - Force device sync in Intune portal
    - Select **Sync Now** from the integration's menu in June
    - Check device connectivity and management status
    - Review Intune device compliance policies
  </Accordion>
  
  <Accordion title="Performance Issues">
    **Symptoms**: Slow syncs or timeouts
    
    **Solutions**:
    - Large tenants take longer to sync. Follow progress in **Sync Status** on the integration's page.
    - If a sync fails, check the **Activity** section on the integration's page for the error and contact June support
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
