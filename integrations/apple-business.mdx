Connect your Apple Business account with June to automatically sync Apple device information, including purchase data and device enrollment details, directly from Apple's Business Manager portal.

## What you'll accomplish
- Set up API integration in Apple Business
- Connect Apple Business to June
- Sync Apple device inventory and purchase data
- Understand data mapping and synchronization

## Prerequisites

Before starting the integration, ensure you have:
- [Apple Business](https://business.apple.com) account with administrator access
- API access enabled in Apple Business
- Apple devices purchased through authorized channels and assigned to your Apple Business organization (see [Apple Business](https://business.apple.com) for more information)

## Key Features

- **Device Synchronization**: Automatically sync all Apple devices from your Apple Business account especially devices that are not enrolled in your MDM.
- **Purchase Data**: Import purchase dates, order numbers, and vendor details, and calculate warranty expiration dates.

## Setup Process

### Step 1: Create API Integration in Apple Business

<Steps>
  <Step title="Access Apple Business">
    1. Log into your Apple Business account at [business.apple.com](https://business.apple.com)
    2. Navigate to **Preferences**
    <img src="/images/abm_manage_preferences.png" alt="Apple Business Preferences" />
    3. Look for the **API** section.
  </Step>
  
  <Step title="Create New API Key">
    1. Click **+** to generate a new API integration
    2. Enter **June Integration** as the API account Label.
    <img src="/images/abm_setup_manage_integration_label.png" alt="Apple Business Create API Integration" />
    3. Click **Create** to generate a new API key.
    4. Click **Generate Private Key** to generate a new private key.
    <img src="/images/abm_manage_generate_key.png" alt="Apple Business Create API Integration" />
    5. The private key will be downloaded to your computer and your integration will be created.
    6. Click **Manage** to manage the API integration.
    7. You should be able to see your Client ID and Key ID. 
    <img src="/images/abm_setup_manage_details.png" alt="Apple Business Create API Integration" />
    
    <Note>
    The API key will be used for JWT-based authentication between June and Apple Business.
    </Note>
  </Step>
  
  <Step title="Download Private Key">
    1. Open the downloaded private key file (`.p8` format) with a text editor.
    2. Note the **Key ID** (10 characters) and **Client ID**
    3. Save these credentials securely - you'll need them for June configuration.
    
    <Warning>
    Private keys cannot be re-downloaded. Store them securely and never share them outside your organization.
    </Warning>
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Step 2: Connect Apple Business in June

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Apple Business integration">
    1. Log into June
    2. Go to **Settings** > **Integrations**
    3. Select **Apple Business Manager** under **Available Integrations** (in the **Mobile Device Management** group)
  </Step>
  
  <Step title="Enter API Credentials">
    Fill in the required authentication fields:
    - **Client ID**: Your Apple Business Client ID
    - **Key ID**: The Key ID associated with your private key
    - **Private Key**: Paste the contents of your `.p8` private key file
    - **Default Warranty Years**: The warranty length that applies to your devices. June adds it to the purchase date from Apple Business to set the warranty expiration date when a device has none yet.
    
    <Warning>
    Ensure the private key is pasted in the correct PEM format, including the header and footer lines.
    </Warning>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Connect to Apple Business">
    1. Click **Connect**
    2. June tests the connection with your credentials
    3. If the test succeeds, June starts the first sync automatically
    4. Each sync appears in the **Activity** section at the bottom of the integration's page
    
    <Check>
    The integration shows **Connected**, and the **Activity** section shows the result of each sync, including how many devices were processed.
    </Check>
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Step 3: Initial Data Sync

<Steps>
  <Step title="Run Initial Sync">
    1. After you connect, the first sync starts automatically.
    2. June imports all device data from your Apple Business account
    3. Check **Sync Status** and **Last Sync** on the integration's page in **Settings** > **Integrations**
    4. Initial sync time depends on the number of devices in your Apple Business account
    5. June syncs again once a day. To sync sooner, open the **Connected** menu at the top of the integration's page and select **Sync Now**.
  </Step>
  
  <Step title="Verify Imported Data">
    Once the sync completes:
    - June creates a new asset for each serial number that is not already in your asset inventory.
    - You can see the purchase details by clicking on an asset and looking at the "Purchase Details" in the **Overview** tab.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## API Authentication

The Apple Business integration uses JWT-based authentication with ECDSA P-256 certificates for secure API access:

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Authentication Method">
    - **Algorithm**: ES256 (ECDSA with SHA-256)
    - **Key Format**: PKCS#8 PEM format (.p8 files)
    - **Automatic Renewal**: Tokens are automatically renewed before expiration
  </Accordion>
  
  <Accordion title="Security Features">
    - Private keys and tokens are encrypted at rest in June's database
    - All API communication uses TLS encryption
    - If you rotate the key in Apple Business, update the Client ID, Key ID and Private Key in June.
    - June only reads data from Apple Business. It never changes anything there.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Data Synchronization

### Device Data Mapping

| Apple Business field | In June | Description |
|-----------|------------|-------------|
| `serialNumber` | Serial number | Device serial number |
| `deviceModel` | Product model | Device model (e.g., "MacBook Pro 13-inch") |
| `productType` | Product SKU | Device SKU (e.g., "iMac16,2") |
| `color` | Product color | Device color |
| `orderDateTime` | Purchase Date | Date device was purchased |
| `orderNumber` | Purchase Order | Order number for the purchase |
| `addedToOrgDateTime` | Added to Organization via ABM | Date device was added to your organization |
| `productFamily` | Operating system and device type | Operating system (macOS, iOS, iPadOS, tvOS, watchOS, visionOS) |
| `purchaseSourceType` | Vendor | Vendor name |

### Sync Behavior

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Initial Sync">
    - **Complete Import**: All devices from Apple Business are imported on first sync
    - **Data Processing**: Purchase data, warranty info, and device specs are mapped
    - **Duration**: Depends on device count
  </Accordion>
  
  <Accordion title="Incremental Updates">
    - **Regular Sync**: June syncs once a day
    - **New Devices**: Automatically imports newly purchased devices
    - **Existing Devices**: Fills in purchase details that are still empty. June does not overwrite purchase details that are already set, including ones you entered.
  </Accordion>
  
  <Accordion title="Duplicate Handling">
    - **Unique Identifier**: Matches devices by serial number
    - **Linked Records**: Each Apple Business device is linked to the June asset with the same serial number
    - **Conflict Resolution**: Purchase details already on the asset are kept. Apple Business only fills in empty fields.
  </Accordion>
  
  <Accordion title="Error Handling">
    - **Failed Syncs**: The integration's page shows **Sync failed**
    - **Details**: The **Activity** section shows the error for each failed sync
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Troubleshooting

### Authentication Issues

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Connection Test Fails">
    **Symptoms**: Connecting or **Test Connection** shows an error
    
    **Solutions**:
    - Verify Client ID, Key ID, and Private Key are correct in June settings
    - Ensure private key is in proper PEM format with headers and footers
    - Check that the key hasn't been revoked or disabled in Apple Business
    - Confirm the API key has appropriate permissions for device data access.
  </Accordion>
  
  <Accordion title="Private Key Not Accepted">
    **Symptoms**: The connection fails right after you paste the private key
    
    **Solutions**:
    - Paste the whole key file, including the `-----BEGIN` and `-----END` lines
    - Ensure no extra whitespace or formatting issues in the key
    - Check that Key ID matches the key in Apple Business
    - Regenerate the API key if the current one is corrupted
  </Accordion>
  
</AccordionGroup>

### Sync Issues

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Sync Fails or Imports No Devices">
    **Symptoms**: The integration shows **Sync failed**, or no devices appear
    
    **Solutions**:
    - Verify your Apple Business account has devices assigned to your organization
    - Check the **Activity** section on the integration's page for the error
    - Run **Sync Now** from the **Connected** menu to try again
  </Accordion>
  
  <Accordion title="Missing or Incorrect Data">
    **Symptoms**: Devices sync but with incomplete purchase data
    
    **Solutions**:
    - Verify purchase information is complete in Apple Business
    - Check that warranty and pricing data is available from the vendor
    - Update device information in Apple Business if editable
    - Contact your Apple reseller about missing purchase data
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Best Practices

### Security
- **Key Management**: Regularly rotate API keys and store them in secure, encrypted storage
- **Access Control**: Limit Apple Business administrator access to authorized personnel only
- **Audit Logging**: Monitor API key usage and access patterns
- **Regular Reviews**: Periodically audit device assignments and API permissions

## Support

Need help with your Apple Business integration?

- **Integration Activity**: Check the **Activity** section on the integration's page in **Settings** > **Integrations** for error details
- **Connection Testing**: Select **Test Connection** from the **Connected** menu on the integration's page
- **Apple Business support**: Contact Apple for issues in Apple Business
- **June Support**: Contact June support with error messages and timestamps

<Note>
Apple Business integration syncs purchase and warranty data automatically. Device management capabilities require a separate MDM integration like Jamf Pro or Microsoft Intune.
</Note>
