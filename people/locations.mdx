Locations lists your offices and warehouses, the people at each, and the assets held there. Admins and Owners can add locations that no integration manages.

## Where locations come from

| Source | Locations |
|---|---|
| [Okta](/integrations/okta) | Each person's work location, as an Office. Without a mapped **Work location source field**, Okta locations are built from city and state |
| [Google Workspace](/integrations/google-workspace) | Workspace buildings, and employee addresses named from the address, such as "Austin, TX" |
| [Microsoft Entra ID](/integrations/microsoft-entra) | Each user's office location, as an Office |
| [Jamf Pro](/integrations/jamf-pro) | Buildings, as Offices |
| [Firstbase](/integrations/firstbase) | Offices as Offices, and warehouses as Warehouses |
| CSV import | Office or Warehouse, as set in the file |
| You | Locations you create here |

Directory sources sync once a day. After you change a location in a source, it shows here at the next sync, or when you select **Sync Now** on the integration's page.

A sync reuses a location whose name matches exactly, including capitalization. "NYC" and "nyc" become two locations. Google Workspace names locations from employee addresses, so they may not match names from other sources.

**If you created a location by hand and a sync later reports the same name, it becomes a synced location.** It then comes from the source and can't be edited or deleted in June. A Google Workspace building with the same name also sets it to Office.

A person's location comes from your identity provider. To change it, change it there. If your identity provider doesn't report a location for someone, Jamf Pro sets it from the device's building. A CSV employee import can also set it, but your identity provider's value comes back on the next sync.

## The Locations list

Columns are **Location** (name and address), **Type**, **Employees**, **Assets** and **Source**. Search by name, and sort by Location, Type, Employees or Assets. **Create Location** is at the top of the page.

## Create a location

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the form">
    Go to **Locations** and select **Create Location**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill in the details">
    Enter a **Name** and choose a **Location Type**: Office (the default) or Warehouse. Add the address if you have it: **Address Line 1**, **Address Line 2**, **Country**, **State / Province**, **City** and **ZIP / Postal Code**. For state and city, pick from a list with **Dropdown**, or type them with **Manual** if yours isn't listed.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Select **Create Location**. The location appears in the list right away.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Names are unique, ignoring capitalization. "Location with the same name already exists" means another location already uses it.

## Edit or delete a location

Open the actions menu next to the location name, in the list or on the location's page, then choose **Edit** or **Delete**. Locations from a source show no menu.

- **Edit** opens the same form. Select **Update Location** to save.
- **Delete** asks you to confirm and cannot be undone. It is unavailable while people or assets are assigned. The menu item says why, for example "Cannot delete: has 2 employees and 1 asset".

To empty a location before deleting it, move its assets: open the assignee popover in the Assets list or on the asset's page, choose **Location**, then **Assign to Location**. For people, change their location in your identity provider.

## Open a location

The header shows the address and sources. **Employees** lists the people at the location, with their assets. **Assets** lists assets assigned to the location itself, such as spare stock in a warehouse. It does not include the devices of people who work there. For those, open the person in [Employees](/people/employees).

## Who can do what

| Role | Open Locations | Add, Edit, Delete Locations |
|---|---|---|
| Owner, Admin | Yes | Yes |
| Member | Yes | No |
| Software Procurement, AI Usage | No | No |

Custom roles use **View Locations**, **Add Locations**, **Edit Locations** and **Delete Locations**, and a role can hold any one of them. The Source column and the sources in a location's header need **Employee identities & groups** and **Integrations**, which only Admin and Owner have among built-in roles. Moving an asset needs permission to update assets, which Owner and Admin have. Roles are set under **Settings > Roles**. See [Members and roles](/admin/roles).

## If something looks wrong

**Edit and Delete are missing.** The location comes from a source, or you created it and a sync later matched its name. Change it in the source.

**Delete is greyed out.** People or assets are still assigned. Move them as described above.

**The same office appears twice.** Sources spelled the name differently (capitalization counts), or Google Workspace created it from an employee address. Fix the name in the source.

**An office shows 0 assets while people there have laptops.** Location assets are assigned to the location, not to people.
