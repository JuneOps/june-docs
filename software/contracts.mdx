A contract is the record of what your organization pays for an application. June uses contracts to calculate spend, cost per license, health status and renewal dates.

Open **Resources > Software > Contracts**.

<Frame caption="The Contracts list">
  <img src="/images/software/contracts-list.png" alt="The Contracts list with cards for Total Spend, Active Contracts, Expiring Soon and Next Renewal, lifecycle tabs, and a Renewal column" />
</Frame>

**Who can do this:** Members can view contracts. Software Procurement, Admin and Owner can also create, edit and delete contracts and payments, and process documents. See [Roles](/admin/roles).

## Where contracts come from

| Source | How it works |
|---|---|
| You | Click **New Contract**, or ask [Agent](/agents/agent) to create one or record a payment |
| [Zip](/integrations/zip) | Each agreement becomes a contract, with Zip's fields and attached documents. June does not extract from Zip documents |
| [Coupa](/integrations/coupa) | Each requisition becomes a contract. June extracts the attached documents when the sync runs |

If June recognizes the product in a Zip or Coupa contract, it adds the app to the [Software inventory](/software/software-inventory) when needed. Otherwise the contract arrives unlinked and appears on **Needs Attention** for you to link. Saving any contract recalculates the app's health status. Creating a contract or changing its end date reschedules its renewal reminders.

## The contracts list

The cards at the top are **Total Spend**, **Active Contracts**, **Expiring Soon** and **Next Renewal**. Click a card to filter the list, or click **Next Renewal** to open that contract. Tabs filter the list, each with a count.

| Tab | Shows |
|---|---|
| **All** | Every contract that is not ignored |
| **Active** | Linked to an app, started, and not past its end date. A contract with no end date stays active |
| **Expiring Soon** | Active contracts ending within 90 days |
| **Expired** | Linked contracts with an end date before today |
| **Future** | Start date after today |
| **Ignored** | Contracts you chose to ignore |
| **Needs Attention** | Not linked to an app, or a document extraction needs review or failed |

A contract that is not linked to an app, or has no start date, is never active, so it is left out of spend. Search with **Search contracts...**. Display settings add **Renewal** (Pending, In review, Approved, Rejected), **App Linking** and **Review Status**, plus view and sort options.

## Add a contract

Only **Software Application** is required. The button needs the **Add Contracts** permission (Software Procurement, Admin, Owner), and it is hidden on the Needs Attention and Ignored tabs.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Start the form">
    Click **New Contract**. From an app's **Contracts** tab, **New Contract** pre-selects the app.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the application">
    In **Software Application**, search for the app. To add one June has not found, type its name and choose **Link to "name"**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the terms">
    Pick a **Contract Type** and **Term**, enter the value, and set **Contract Validity**. **From** and **To** default to today and 12 months from today. Add **Number of Licenses**, **Billing frequency** and **Payment Method** if you know them. For renewal details, see [Software renewals](/software/software-renewals).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create it">
    Click **Create Contract**. June opens the app's Contracts tab.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The **Summary** panel shows Monthly Cost, Annual Cost and Cost Per license as you type.

### Contract types and terms

| If you pay for... | Contract Type | Term | Enter |
|---|---|---|---|
| A fixed period, such as 12 months | Software Subscription (default) | Fixed term | **Total contract value**, a **Billing frequency** and dates |
| A rolling monthly fee with no end | Software Subscription | Recurring | **Price per month**. Leave the To date empty |
| A committed minimum plus overage | Usage-Based / Pay-As-You-Go | Recurring (locked) | The committed monthly base, which can be \$0. Record payments for the real cost |
| Unlimited seats | Site License | Fixed term | Value and dates. License count shows as Unlimited |
| A one-time purchase | Perpetual License | Fixed term | Value. No To date |

Other types are Add-On / Feature Module, Enterprise Agreement, Free / Open Source, Support Contract, Trial / Evaluation and True-Up Agreement.

Fixed-term monthly cost is the total value divided by the contract length in months, and annual cost is monthly times 12. Recurring contracts bill monthly.

### Record payments

Open the contract, click **Edit contract**, and use **Payments > Add payment**. Enter **Paid on**, **Amount** and an optional note. The section shows **Recorded spend** and **By month**. Edit or delete a payment from its row.

For a usage-based contract, monthly cost is the most recent month with a recorded payment, or the base if there are none. Spend to date counts recorded payments, and the base for months with no payment.

## Fill a contract from a document

Uploading a document stores it. It does not fill any fields until you process it. Save the contract first, then:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Upload the document">
    In **Documents**, click **Upload document**. PDF, Word, Excel and image files up to 10 MB are accepted.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Process it">
    Click the **Process document** icon on the file. The status shows Processing, and the form refreshes with "Contract updated from latest extraction". Extraction reads fields such as dates, value, licenses, billing frequency, auto-renew and notice period.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
**Process document** and **Re-extract from documents** do not work on Zip contracts. Enter the values yourself, or use **Re-sync contract data** to pull Zip's fields again.
</Warning>

Coupa contracts do not need this step: **Document extraction history** on the contract shows what was read and any errors. On Coupa contracts, **More actions** has **Re-sync contract data** and **Re-extract from documents**. Use Re-extract for documents added in Coupa after the first import.

## Fix a contract that needs attention

Open the **Needs Attention** tab. The Review column explains each one.

- **Unlinked:** click **Link to application** and pick the app.
- **Needs review** or **Failed:** open the contract, click **Edit contract**, fill in the missing fields and click **Update Contract**. It clears once start date, end date, value, type and license count are filled in.

## Ignore or delete

| Action | Where | What happens | Undo |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Ignore** | Row **More actions**, or the contract's **More actions** | Hides the contract from every tab except Ignored. It stops counting toward spend, health status and the renewal signal | **Restore** on the Ignored tab |
| **Delete** | Contract's **More actions** | Removes the contract and cancels its renewal reminders | None |

Use Ignore for duplicates or imports you may want back. Reminders already scheduled for an ignored contract still send, so delete the contract to cancel them.

## If something looks wrong

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="A contract is missing from Total Spend">
    It is unlinked, has no start date, starts in the future, has expired, or is ignored.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="An uploaded document did not fill in the contract">
    Upload stores the file. Save the contract, then click **Process document**.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
