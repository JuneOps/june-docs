---
title: 'AI limit requests'
description: 'How employees ask for a higher monthly AI tool limit, how to set up the policy that decides, and how IT decides in June.'
---

An AI limit request is an employee asking for a higher monthly limit on Claude or ChatGPT. Approvers or IT decide, June raises the limit, and every ask and change is kept in a history. The limit itself, and changing it directly, is covered in [Spend limits](/ai-usage/spend-limits).

## How employees ask

| Where | How | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| Inside Claude Enterprise or ChatGPT Enterprise | The employee uses the tool's own "request more usage" button. | June picks the ask up within a few minutes and lists it as Pending. IT decides it in June or in the provider's console. It doesn't go through limit policies. |
| Slack | The employee messages June. | June files the ask, then follows the limit policy that covers them. |
| Jira | The employee opens a ticket assigned to June. | Same as Slack. |

For Slack and Jira, the employee needs an active seat on the tool. Naming an amount is optional. If the policy requires a reason, June asks for it before filing. If the person asks again while an ask is waiting, June adds it to the waiting ask. A Jira ticket replaces a waiting Slack ask.

## Set up a limit policy

A limit policy decides what happens to Slack and Jira asks: who approves, how much the limit goes up, and for how long.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the app">
    Go to **Access** > **Policies** and open the app for the tool. Limit policies sit on the same page as its access policies.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose who it covers">
    June uses the one limit policy that covers the employee for that tool. If none covers them, or two do, the ask goes to IT.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the reason prompt and duration">
    Optionally require a business reason. The default prompt is "Why do you need more usage?". Choose a duration of **Permanent** or **Until end of month**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add tiers">
    A policy has 1 to 5 tiers, chosen by how many raises the person has had approved this month. Tier 1 covers the first raise, tier 2 the second, and the last tier applies to every later raise.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Each tier sets:

- **Approval:** none, or a chain of the employee's manager and named people.
- **Amount:** increase by a percentage, increase by a fixed amount, set to an amount, or approver decides.
- **A maximum** for the new limit.

For example, tier 1 could need no approval and add 25% up to \$500, while tier 2 needs the manager and sets the limit to an amount they choose, up to \$2,000.

## How June decides

| Situation | What happens |
|---|---|
| Tier needs no approval | June raises the limit automatically. The Actor column shows "Automatically via June". |
| Tier needs approval | Approvers get a Slack card, then June raises the limit once they approve. |
| Several approvers approve different amounts | June raises to the lowest amount approved. Later approvers can approve up to that amount, or reject. |
| No policy covers the person, or two do | The ask goes to IT with a note. |
| No manager, an inactive or unreachable approver, or the rule doesn't give a higher limit | The ask goes to IT with a note that says why and how to avoid it. |
| The provider doesn't confirm a raise | June retries up to four more times over about 15 minutes, then sends the ask to IT. |

June changes a limit in three cases only: a person with permission approves or saves a change, a limit policy you set up runs, or a temporary raise ends and June puts back the earlier limit. It never raises above an approver's amount or a tier's maximum on its own, and never applies a raise twice: it checks the provider's current limit before each retry. IT deciding in June can go above a tier's maximum.

## Decide a request in June

Open **AI usage**, choose **Claude** or **OpenAI** (ChatGPT), then the **Limit requests** tab. When an ask goes to IT, June's note links straight to it.

  <img src="/images/help-desk/ai-limit-requests-list.png" alt="Limit requests table with a search box and a count of 9 requests. Columns are Employee, Source, Monthly limit, Status and Actor. Every row has Source Claude. The first row is Pending with usage at 100% of its limit and a dash as Actor; the rows below are Approved or Declined, with Actor values such as 'Maya Patel · via console' and 'API · via console'." />
</Frame>

Columns are **Employee**, **Source**, **Monthly limit**, **Status** and **Actor**. Search by employee, and use the display settings to sort or filter by status.

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Pending | Waiting for a decision. |
| Approved | The limit was raised. |
| Declined | The ask was declined. |
| Canceled | The request was closed before a decision, or the provider canceled it. |
| Superseded | ChatGPT replaced the ask with a later one. |
| Unknown | The provider reported a status June doesn't recognize. June treats the ask as open. |

The **Actor** column shows who decided and where: a name with "via June", "via console" or "via Slack", or "Automatically via June" when no approval was needed. Hover for the decision reason. Asks nobody has decided yet show "-", as do Canceled and Superseded asks.

**Who can do this:** Owner and Admin (the **Change AI spending limits** permission). Members and the AI Usage role can view the list but not decide.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the ask">
    Click the row. The panel shows the person's current limit, their default, what they asked for, and their reason. A warning icon beside a name means June couldn't match the requester; see below.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the limit">
    **New limit** is prefilled with the amount they asked for. Quick chips add 25%, 50% or 100% to the current limit. To add a fixed amount, type the current limit plus that amount. If a policy sets the amount, leave it empty to raise by the policy's rule.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose how long">
    **Duration** is **Indefinite** (shown as Permanent in Slack) or **Until end of month**. When a policy handles the ask, its duration applies.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Approve or decline">
    To approve, click **Approve**. A reason is optional. To decline, type a reason and click **Decline**. For Slack and Jira asks, the employee sees the reason.
  </Step>
</Steps>

June refuses a limit more than 10 times the current one (\$1,000 for a \$100 limit). A decision takes effect right away. To undo it, change the limit from **Employee usage** ([Spend limits](/ai-usage/spend-limits)).

To cancel a Slack or Jira ask, open the matching request in [Requests](/help-desk/requests) and set it to **Canceled**. That is the only status change available there for a limit ask. Closing the ask's Jira ticket does the same. The employee is told their request was canceled. Asks from inside Claude or ChatGPT have no request in June; decline them instead.

## Decide in Slack

Approvers named in a policy tier get a DM card showing who is asking, their usage this month (for example "\$150 of \$200 used this month (75%)"), raises already approved this month, the amount and duration the policy gives, and the reason.

They click **Approve** or **Reject**. On an "approver decides" tier, Approve opens a form to pick the new limit: the employee's ask, 25%, 50% or 100% more, or another amount, within the tier's maximum. Reject asks for a reason the employee will see (up to 500 characters). Approvers don't need a June role.

## What the employee sees

For Slack and Jira asks, June answers where the employee asked. Asks from inside Claude or ChatGPT get no message from June.

| Moment | Message |
|---|---|
| Approved | "Approved. I'm raising your [tool] limit now and will let you know here when it's done." Sent only when the tier had approvers. |
| Raised | "Your [tool] limit is now \$X a month", with ", until the end of the month" when temporary. For ChatGPT the amount reads "N credits a month". |
| Declined | "[Decider] declined your request" followed by the reason, if there is one. |
| Canceled | "Your request for more [tool] usage was canceled." |

## Temporary raises and history

A raise set to **Until end of month** ends at the end of the calendar month, UTC, and June puts back the earlier limit. See [Spend limits](/ai-usage/spend-limits#temporary-raises) for the revert rules. Every ask and change stays in the **Limit requests** list, and the employee's profile shows a **Limit history** card on its AI usage tab.

## If something looks wrong

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="A warning icon sits beside a name">
    June couldn't match the requester to anyone in your directory. Check the account before approving.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Approve or Decline is missing">
    Your role can only view limit requests, or an Owner has blocked the action for your role.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Approve says to lower a limit, or that it is already their limit">
    Approve needs a limit above the current one. To keep or lower it, use **Decline**.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="June couldn't confirm the raise">
    The note to IT says the provider didn't respond. Approve again to have June try again, or decline to close the ask. Approving again is safe.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## FAQ

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Can employees ask for more Cursor?">
    No. Cursor has no Limit requests tab and June doesn't take Cursor asks. IT can still change a Cursor limit from **Employee usage**.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What if I lower someone's limit while they have an open ask?">
    The button reads **Lower and decline**, and the ask is declined with the change. Otherwise the ask stays open.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
