---
title: 'Ticket context'
description: 'See who asked and the details IT needs about the person a ticket is for, as an internal note on the Jira ticket.'
---

When a ticket arrives in the Jira project June tracks, June adds an internal note saying who asked. For onboarding, offboarding, device, sign-in, AI usage and software access tickets, it also adds the details IT needs about the person the ticket is for, such as a new hire's start date and accounts, or someone's devices and when they last checked in.

June finds that person from the ticket's title, an @-mention or an email address. When it's less sure, it marks them "please confirm". When it can't tell, it says so rather than guess. If June later works out who the ticket is for, or that it's for someone else, it adds a short follow-up. The ticket also shows on that person's page in June.

The note is internal, so requesters don't see it. June adds notes only in Jira Service Management projects.

## Turn it on

**Who can do this:** Admin and Owner.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Connect Jira and register the webhook">
    Follow [Jira](/integrations/jira) to connect Jira, then [register the webhook](/integrations/jira#webhook-configuration) with a JQL filter that includes your IT project.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter the project key">
    Under **Settings** > **Integrations** > **Jira**, enter your IT project's key in **Default Project Key**, for example `IT`.
  </Step>
</Steps>

To turn it off, switch off **Automatically enrich tickets** in the **Webhooks** section of the Jira integration page.

## If something looks wrong

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="A note is missing or names the wrong person">
    Check the project key and the webhook's JQL filter. Announcements and closed tickets get no note. For the person, put their full name in the title, @-mention them, or include their email address. June matches the reporter by email, then by full name, so make sure the Jira account's email is visible or its name matches your directory.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
