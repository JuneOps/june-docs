Assets is June's inventory of devices and peripherals. Find a device, see who has it and what state it is in, and open its detail page for activity, purchase data and history.

Owners and Admins can view and edit. Members can view and export. Roles without asset access (Software Procurement, AI Usage) do not see Assets in the sidebar. See [Roles](/admin/roles).

## How assets get into June

There is no Add button. Assets arrive from the systems you connect and from files you import.

| Way in | What it brings |
|---|---|
| Device management: [Jamf Pro](/integrations/jamf-pro), [Kandji](/integrations/kandji), [Microsoft Intune](/integrations/microsoft-intune) | Managed devices, their user and their security details |
| [Apple Business Manager](/integrations/apple-business-manager) | Devices bought through Apple, with purchase dates |
| [Firstbase](/integrations/firstbase) | Managed devices only: computers, phones, tablets and Apple TVs. Accessories are skipped |
| [CSV import](/device-management/asset-upload) | Devices and peripherals, including costs and dates |
| Chat with June | Peripherals (monitors, keyboards, mice, docks, headsets, headphones) created from an existing catalog product, 1 to 50 at a time |

Each serial number is unique in your organization. A peripheral with no serial gets a generated ID that starts with `JUNE-`. When these sources disagree about a device, see [Asset sources](/assets/asset-sources).

## The dashboard

The dashboard opens with four tiles: **Total assets**, **Average age**, **Total cost** and **Compliance**. Below them are the **Asset Discovery Over Time** chart, a **Needs Attention** card with **Lost Assets** and **Refresh due soon** (the next 90 days), and charts of assets by department, location and status.

- **Total cost** shows to roles with **Asset Procurement & Depreciation** access, which Members have.
- **Compliance** shows to roles with **Device Compliance & management details** access, which Members have. It reads "Not configured" until you [set up compliance](/assets/compliance).

## All Assets

Go to **Assets > All Assets**.

The list opens with a default **Status** filter that hides four statuses. Click **Display** and add them under **Status** to see those devices. [Asset status](/assets/asset-status) lists which four and why. Your filter choices stay in your browser.

- **Search** has two modes. Basic matches text. Smart takes plain language, such as "assets assigned to Bob" or "devices created in the last 30 days".
- **Display** filters by Status, Ownership, Compliance, Classification, Platform, Condition, Last Check In and Department.
- **Columns** are Asset, Serial Number, Assignee, Condition, OS, Status, Compliance, Last Check In and Source. All but Compliance and Source sort.
- **Export** downloads the rows you are looking at, filters and search included. It needs **Export assets & products** plus view access to costs and security details. Members have all three.

Click a Status, Assignee or Condition cell to edit it in place (Owners and Admins). Tick rows, shift-click for a range, then use **Update Status**, **Update Condition** and **Apply** to change many at once. An unlocked status can be replaced by a later sync, see [Asset status](/assets/asset-status).

### Reassign a device

1. Click the **Assignee** cell, or **Assigned To** on the detail page.
2. Click **Assign Device**, **Re-assign Device** or **Unassign Device**.
3. Choose **Employee** or **Location** and search.
4. Click **Update Assignment** in **Confirm Assignment Change**.

If the device is in an MDM, June updates the MDM first, and the dialog tells you so. If the MDM returns an error, nothing changes in June. If the MDM does not have the device, June saves the assignment and sets Awaiting Enrollment.

Assigning to a location removes the person in Kandji and Intune, and in Jamf when the location is one of its buildings. Assigning to a person turns Available, Other or blank into Deployed. Assigning to a location turns Deployed, Other or blank into Available. See [Employees](/people/employees) and [Locations](/people/locations).

## Asset detail

Click a row to open the asset. The header shows **Assigned To**, **Last Check In**, **Status**, **Condition** and **Sources**.

<Frame caption="An asset's Overview tab: header, tabs, summary tiles, Device Details, Compliance and Purchase Details.">
  <img src="/images/assets/asset-detail-overview.png" alt="The Overview tab of an asset showing the header with Assigned To, Last Check In, Status, Condition and Sources, the tab row, tiles for Purchase Cost, Warranty Expiry, Installed Apps, Refresh Status and Operating System, and the Device Details, Compliance and Purchase Details cards" />
</Frame>

| Tab | What it shows |
|---|---|
| **Overview** | Device Details, Purchase Details, Compliance, Sources, and a history of status, assignment and ownership changes |
| **Device Activity** | Check-in details, device groups, and recovery keys and unlock codes for people allowed to reveal them. Devices only |
| **Procurement** | Orders, returns and tracking. See [Orders and returns](/assets/orders-and-returns) |
| **Refresh** | Refresh status, policy and timeline. Devices only |
| **Depreciation** | Current value and schedule. Devices only |
| **Applications** | Installed apps with usage. Devices only |
| **Tickets** | Requests linked to the device |
| **Notes & Docs** | Notes and uploaded documents |

Peripherals hide Device Activity, Refresh, Depreciation and Applications, and are never assessed for compliance.

Click **Ownership** in Device Details to set Corporate, Personal, Other or Unknown. Your choice is kept over what the MDM reports.

### Remove a device from its MDM

Open the "..." menu on the detail page, click **Remove from MDM**, and confirm **Remove Device**. The menu item appears only for devices in an MDM, and only for Owners and Admins (**Delete assets & Refresh Policies**). June never removes a device on its own.

## Product Catalog

Go to **Assets > Product Catalog**. A product is a model, such as a laptop line, that assets belong to. The list shows Active products by default; use **Display** to show Archived ones or filter by Manufacturer.

<Frame caption="The Product Catalog: name, SKU, manufacturer, asset count, price and status for each product.">
  <img src="/images/assets/product-catalog.png" alt="The Product Catalog list with columns Name, SKU, Manufacturer, Assets, Price, Updated, Status and Actions" />
</Frame>

Products are created by your connected sources and by chat; there is no New product button. In a row's menu, **Add price** and **Edit price** open a dialog with Price, Effective From and an optional Effective To. **Archive product** and **Activate product** change its status.

A price must be above zero, prices for one product cannot overlap, and only one can be open-ended. A price change updates the catalog price on every asset of that product. An asset with no real cost shows its product's catalog price, labeled "Catalog price", or "Catalog price · June AI estimate" for an Apple product with no price.

## If something looks wrong

| You see | Why |
|---|---|
| A Mac shows an unknown platform | Platform comes from the OS name, else from the product name. A computer neither identifies stays unknown |
| A status you expect is missing from the picker | The picker offers only statuses that fit the assignee. See [Asset status](/assets/asset-status) |
| **Condition** cannot be clicked | The device has no condition data yet |

## FAQ

<AccordionGroup>
<Accordion title="How do I add a monitor or dock?">
Ask June in chat to create it from a catalog product, or import a CSV. The product must already exist in the Product Catalog.
</Accordion>
<Accordion title="When does a change take effect?">
A status, assignee or condition you change in June applies immediately, and an assignment is written to the MDM at once. What your integrations report arrives with their next sync, see [Asset status](/assets/asset-status).
</Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
