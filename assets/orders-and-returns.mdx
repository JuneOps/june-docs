---
title: 'Orders and returns'
description: 'See device orders, returns and tracking from your logistics provider, and what June does when a device ships or comes back.'
---

When you connect a logistics provider such as [Firstbase](/integrations/firstbase), June reads its orders and returns and shows them next to the device and the person.

June does not place orders or start returns. It does not remove a device from your MDM when someone leaves: **Remove from MDM** on the asset is a manual action. Decisions such as legal hold or what to do with damaged devices stay with you, and you can automate your own policy with a [runbook](/agents/runbooks).

Orders and returns show on the **Procurement** tab, which Owners, Admins and Members can open. Assigning a device from a signal's suggestion needs an Owner or Admin. See [Roles](/admin/roles).

## The Procurement tab

Open an asset and click **Procurement**.

{/* screenshot: /assets/[id] | Procurement tab of a device with an onboarding order and a return | crop to stat boxes and Order History | file: /images/assets/orders-and-returns-procurement.png */}

| Box | Shows |
|---|---|
| **Deploy Status** | The provider's own stage for the device, with its reason on hover. June's status is in the header |
| **Condition** | The provider's condition grade |
| **Reported Assignee** | Who the provider says has it, or "Provider warehouse: [name]" |
| **Open Return** | Yes or No |
| **Source** | The provider. Members do not see this box |

**Order History** lists one row per order or return, labeled for example "Order · Onboarding", "Return · Offboarding" or "Order · Replacement". Each row shows its status and date, who it is for, the return reason, and the serial. Click **Track via [carrier]** to open tracking, or copy the tracking code.

A return sent with no retrieval arranged has no tracking. An order line has no device until the provider assigns a serial. If the tab is empty, it says "No procurement data" (no provider reports this device) or "No order history".

## Ship a laptop to a new hire

1. Place the order in your logistics provider.
2. June picks it up in the next sync, see [Asset status](/assets/asset-status). It appears on the device's Procurement tab once the item has a serial.
3. Until the device enrolls in device management, June shows it as **Awaiting Enrollment** with the new hire as assignee, as long as the new hire is an employee in June.
4. As soon as it enrolls, it becomes **Deployed** with the new hire. If your device management tool later names a different user, that user wins.

When Signals is turned on for your organization, June also raises an **Onboarding scheduled** signal for the new hire, found under **Signals** in the sidebar. It lists their devices and access, and while they have no device it suggests assigning one in June and in your MDM.

## Get a laptop back from a leaver

1. Start the return in your logistics provider.
2. June shows the return and its tracking. The device becomes **Pending Return** and keeps the leaver as assignee.
3. When the provider's warehouse receives the device, June removes the leaver and assigns the device to the provider's warehouse. Restocked devices become **Available**, repairs **In Repair**, and anything else **Pending Return** until restocked. A device under a data hold keeps its status. The full list is on [Asset sources](/assets/asset-sources).

With Signals on, June also raises an **Offboarding scheduled** signal for the leaver. It stays open past the last day until the person holds no devices and no managed app access, and it can suggest moving a returned device to the warehouse location.

## When the signals appear

| Signal | Opens | Due |
|---|---|---|
| Onboarding scheduled | When the start date is within 90 days | 3 days after the start date |
| Offboarding scheduled | When the leave date is within 90 days | 14 days after the leave date |

Before the start date, Onboarding scheduled stays open even if the person has a device. After the start date it resolves once the person has a device, or when the due date passes. Change the due days in **Settings > Organization Policies**. Hire and leave dates come from [Employees](/people/employees).

## If something looks wrong

| You see | Why |
|---|---|
| The return was delivered but the leaver is still the assignee | June unassigns when the provider's warehouse holds the device. Until the warehouse records it, the device stays Pending Return with the person |
| The Offboarding scheduled signal is still open | The person still holds a device or managed app access |
| No tracking link on a return | The return was created without a retrieval method |
| An accessory order is missing | June reads managed devices only, not accessory-only orders |
| The provider shipped a device but nothing shows on Procurement | The order line needs a serial, then the next sync |

## FAQ

<AccordionGroup>
<Accordion title="What can I ask June about orders?">
You can ask June in chat to list orders, returns and inventory by person or serial, and to flag orders that were declined or have a delivery exception, and returns that were not returned.
</Accordion>
<Accordion title="Why does Procurement show a different status from the header?">
Procurement shows the provider's own stage for the device. The header shows June's status, which follows the order on [Asset status](/assets/asset-status).
</Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
