The audit log is a record of changes made in your organization. Use it to answer "who changed this, and when?" during a security review or an investigation.

<Note>
  The audit log is available on the Enterprise plan. Owner and Admin can open **Settings > Audit Log** by default, and any custom role can be given the **Audit Log** permission.
</Note>

## What is recorded

June records when something is created, updated or deleted. That covers the records you manage in June, including:

- Members, roles, role assignments and role permission changes
- Employees, departments and locations
- Assets, products, refresh policies and depreciation settings
- Software contracts, subscriptions and access policies
- Runbooks and custom tools
- Integrations, SCIM users and groups, and organization settings

Changes that integrations make while syncing, and changes that SCIM makes, show **June AI** as the actor. Sign-ins and permission-denied attempts are not recorded.

### Actions by the Agent and runbooks

When the Agent or a runbook changes a record in June, such as an asset's status or an employee's details, the change is recorded here like any other. In a chat, the actor is the person whose message or approval led to the change. If no June user is tied to it, the actor is June AI. A runbook's changes show June AI or the person who started the run, and a scheduled run shows June AI or the person who saved that version of the runbook.

Actions in your connected apps, such as adding someone to an Okta group or sending a Slack message, are not recorded in the audit log. Neither are Approve and Reject decisions. Find both in the chat or in **Runbooks > Run history**, where each approval card shows who decided and when. For an employee request, the request shows each approver's decision.

## Find an entry

Go to **Settings > Audit Log**. Entries are listed newest first, 20 at a time, and more load as you scroll.

Filter by record type with **Events for**, or by person with **Updates by**. Use both together. They default to **Any type** and **Any user**. If nothing matches, the list says "No audit logs found" and names the filters you chose.

To find who changed someone's role, set **Events for** to the role assignments record type and scan the Actor and Date columns. **Events for** takes one record type at a time. Role assignments made before people could hold several roles appear under **Organization Memberships**.

To list what your integrations and SCIM changed, set **Updates by** to **June AI**. There is no date filter, so scroll to the day you need.

You can't export the audit log from the app.

## Read an entry

| Column | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Event** | The kind of record and what happened to it, such as "SoftwareSubscription update" |
| **Actor** | Who made the change |
| **Date** | When it happened, such as "Oct 29, 2026 3:42 PM" |
| **IP Address** | Where the change came from. A dash means none was recorded |

Click a row to open **Audit Details**. **Audit Information** shows the actor, event, date, entity type and entity ID. **Changes Made** shows the changed fields as raw data, each with its old and new value. A created record lists its starting values.

## How long records are kept

Audit records are available for 90 days in the app. For older audits, contact support@juneops.com.

## If something looks wrong

| What you see | Why | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| **Audit Log** is missing from Settings | Your plan is not Enterprise, or your role lacks the **Audit Log** permission | Ask your Owner. See [Members and roles](/admin/roles) |
| Many rows show June AI as the actor | Integration syncs and SCIM changes run as June AI | Set **Updates by** to a person to see only that person's changes |
| "No audit logs found" | Nothing matches the filters, or the change is older than 90 days | Clear a filter, or contact support for older audits |
